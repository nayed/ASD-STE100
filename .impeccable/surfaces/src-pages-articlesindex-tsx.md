# A–Z index (src/pages/ArticlesIndex.tsx)

Scope: /articles, every article by title. Mode: Read/Operate: the reader scans for a title and opens it. An extension of the 404 page's world (src/pages/NotFound.tsx): same document, same paper, same accent rules; no new identity.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The index is the wiki's alphabetical index, the second sheet of the controlled document that the 404 page opens. It refuses the default card list with pill tags and blue links.

OWN-WORLD: Shared with the 404 page (Sheet.tsx, sheet.css): IBM Plex Mono, a datasheet's cold white (#f5f8f9), blue-black ink, cool olive-gray labels, petrol (#006d77) only for the tab of the group on screen and focus; ink change bars; ruled form strip; square corners.

STORY: The reader sees how many articles exist, jumps to a letter with the thumb index or scrolls, and opens an entry; change bars show what changed in the latest revision.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Form strip (IDX-01, revision, pages, letters in use); "All articles" display title, "articles" outlined; STE lede with the / search hint; then the first letter groups. Thumb index: a sticky column of 27 tabs at the right edge from 1024 px (ink tabs for letters in use, faint ones for the rest), a sticky row of the letters in use under the site bar below.

FORM: Extension of the pinned 404 direction (no roll). Signature move: the thumb index, the letter tabs of a printed manual, with the active tab in petrol; entries set as a printed index, title, dot leader, address. seed: none (direction pinned by the user; no roll)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
